# Stream Wall

**Watch up to four live streams at once on one screen, on Windows.**

Stream Wall arranges Twitch, YouTube, TV services and IPTV channels into a single wall of video.
Pick your streams, press **Launch**, and they fill the screen. The main stream has the sound;
one click on another stream makes it the main one.

## Download

Get **`StreamWall-Setup.exe`** from the [latest release](https://github.com/SlimThomas/stream-wall-releases/releases/latest)
and run it. It installs for your Windows user only (no administrator rights needed).

The installer is not code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may say *"Windows protected your PC"*.
Choose **More info → Run anyway**.

**Updates:** when a newer version is out, Stream Wall says so on its setup screen. Download the
new installer and run it; it installs over the old version and keeps your settings and logins.

## Requirements

- Windows 10 or 11
- Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge
- For IPTV: a subscription with an M3U link or an Xtream login (the video player is included)

## Features

- **Layouts:** one big stream with the rest beside it, two big with the rest below, or a grid.
  Focus mode lets the main stream fill the screen.
- **Sound follows the main stream;** the others play muted or softly, with a volume per stream.
- **Live status and previews** for Twitch and YouTube favorites.
- **Logins kept per stream,** so services like TV 2 Play work after logging in once.
- **IPTV:** search an M3U list or an Xtream login by channel name.
- **On the wall:** drag streams to swap them, reload or replace one, add one, show the main
  stream's Twitch chat, take a screenshot.
- **Share a wall** with friends as a short code.
- **Stream Wall Wrapped:** monthly watch-time statistics, stored only on your PC.

## Privacy and fair use

- **Nothing about you is sent anywhere.** Stream Wall has no account, no tracking and no ads.
  Settings, logins and statistics stay on your PC, and IPTV logins are encrypted with your
  Windows login. Stream Wall only connects to the streams you pick, the sites' public pages
  (to see who is live), Twitch's and YouTube's preview pictures, and GitHub once per start (to
  see whether a newer version is out).
- **Bring your own legal streams.** Stream Wall includes no channels or playlists. Use a legal
  IPTV subscription: watching pirated IPTV is illegal in Denmark and many other countries.
- **Follow the sites' rules.** Sites open in a normal browser window, with their ads as they
  are; Stream Wall never blocks or hides them.
